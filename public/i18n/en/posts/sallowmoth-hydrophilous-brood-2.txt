The tireless multi-platformer Garry Brents, with the addition of drummer Allen Gingerich to Sallow Moth, shifts the project towards brutal death metal. The output is densely packed with extreme music of the highest standard (as always with Garry in all of his projects), thematically a development of the strange fantasy universe of the previous release about interdimensional breaches, powerful artifacts, mutations, and so on. Nothing is clear, but very interesting.
