Only the second album in Shygirl’s discography, which can hardly be called a breakthrough - the artist is already widely known, from collabs with Charli XCX to remixes from Björk to her work. But it seems that the album will become iconic - it is a very multi-layered, visionary pop, more complex in design than that of its colleagues. The kind of recordings that, if possible, are listened to and dissected track by track.
