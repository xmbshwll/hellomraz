The most talented psychedelic dream pop of the 90s with shoegaze, post-punk and ambient (and on this record - with a large share of trip hop) is a foursome from Los Angeles with the most politicized long-play in their discography to date. Despite songs about total surveillance, the devaluation of art in the age of AI (sorry Mr. Trump, SI) and the not particularly improving state of the environment, the record creates a wonderful timeless and abstract mood.
