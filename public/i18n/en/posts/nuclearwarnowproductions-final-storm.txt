Cult Canadians, most of whose reputation, since the 80s of the last century, as befits a real black metal band, rested on a small number of crude demos and rapid disintegration. Marco Branco, the band's founding guitarist, also appeared in Blasphemy, which added even more cult status to Witches Hammer. 

As a result, the band reunited in 2018, released their debut album in 2020, and with this album Final Storm apparently finally ends its existence. The album is excellent - it is the band's signature, extremely fast blacked-out thrash in the spirit of Slayer. This is sorely lacking right now.
