And don’t let the cover fool you - this is not an album of children’s songs based on Tolkien about the Shire and the sweet life of hobbits, who, as you know, “love peace, quiet and good plowed land... and, of course, they really appreciate good cuisine and beer.” Beneath the cute pony lies one of the most interesting blackgaze releases of the year so far - with chiptune, breakbeats and, at certain points, flirtations with indie pop. Even more impressive considering that Lammoth is a one-man project from a North Carolina native.
