The new album from the solo project of Curran Reynolds, better known to the general public as the drummer of Today Is The Day, Wetnurse and several other respected bands. Music media often describe it as “as if Bruce Springsteen were playing hardcore,” which is generally not far from the truth - this is a rather strange, but with great knowledge of the matter and hardware (especially punk/metal hardware) classic rock played. For the release, the format of the band changed a little - Curran no longer plays all the instruments himself, Andee Blacksugar plays along on guitar.
