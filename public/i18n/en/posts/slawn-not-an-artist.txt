Blackhaine earlier this year appeared on the debut album of another person who initially came into contact with the music only tangentially - British visual artist and designer with Nigerian roots Olaolu Akeredolu-Ale. The longplay was recorded during a month of studio sessions, during which Slawn’s friends also came to see him - the album consists almost entirely of features, including Skepta’s appearance on one of the tracks. A medley of everything possible - drill, grime, spooken word skits, afrobeat.
