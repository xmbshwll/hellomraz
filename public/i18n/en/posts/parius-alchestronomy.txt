New album from great conceptual prog/death metallers from Philadelphia. As always, the recording is thematic and subordinated to a single end-to-end plot - in this case, it is a progressive opera in the form of a dialogue between two seekers of eternal life, personifying different schools of thought - an alchemist and an astronomer. This time there is much more progress in the sound, the influence of Dream Theater and, a little unexpectedly, Mars Volta is clearly noticeable.
