The son of John Herndon (the founder and one of the permanent drummers of the great Tortoise) has long been leading a successful musical career, spun off from eminent parents who gave him licensed Ableton about 10 years ago. The 22-year-old artist arrives at the peak of his current form for his fifth (!) long play - he has an extensive fanatical fan base and, finally, the most complete material at the moment, which has moved away from flirting with hip-hop towards hyperpop. It seems like it was time to stop raping a long time ago - 2hollis always did best with bright, sparkling electronics.
