A new album from a conceptual anonymous American noise project with a self-explanatory title, both for the band itself and for each of its releases - since the late 2000s, the discography has included releases about the Allied bombing of Dresden, about the tragedy in Chernobyl, about Yugoslavia and Sarajevo in 1992, about the bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, and so on and so forth. Harsh noise with clippings and sounds from media articles about the events and various documentary chronicles for the release about the two Iraq wars becomes even worse - speeches of American preachers about the exceptional historical role of the United States justifying the invasion are often used as audio.
