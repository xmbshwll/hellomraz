An additional EP companion to the third album from the critically acclaimed Will Westerman - A Jackal's Wedding. Since the breakthrough An Inbuilt Fault, Westerman has parted ways with his team and was allegedly unable to capitalize on the buzz around his work, which did not stop him, however, three years later from returning with the most diverse release in his discography - recorded on the Greek island with the romantic name Hydra. The EP complements the album well - this is again very innovative and breakthrough music for the stagnant indie pop genre, with great attention to detail and homage to the classic art rock of the 80s (and we respect rock).
