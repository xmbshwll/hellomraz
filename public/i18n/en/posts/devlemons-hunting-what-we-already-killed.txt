The second album of the new project of the most interesting Philadelphian, often compared with the now extremely popular Nathan Fielder (whom Emmett Kai herself readily admits her love for) - an absurd sense of humor, constantly demonstrated on the Dev Limes YouTube channel, strange transformations, a comedy-parody rap project Queef Jerky. Dev Lemons, despite Emmett's inherent postmodernism and obvious inspiration from the PC Music school of sound, is perhaps her most serious musical project, both in form and content. One of the most interesting pop records of the year.
