The return of the ever-changing Crowhurst project with probably the jazziest and most cinematic album in the discography. Initially, the solo electronic project of the Philadelphia artist began to constantly change from the year 14 - at different stages Crowhurst included (or collaborated with) members of Oxbow, Primitive Man, Daughters and so on. 

On this recording, for example, Dima Dudko from White Ward plays saxophone.
