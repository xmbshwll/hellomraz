A new album from one of the projects of the great and very productive multi-instrumentalist Jason Konen, inventor and popularizer of the term darkjazz - in fact, a mixture of avant-garde jazz with neoclassical music. This time we are not touching on the theme of space horror; on the contrary, the album is dedicated to a painting by Jan Baen from 1675, now hanging in the Reichsmuseum in Amsterdam. It depicts the tragedy of 1672: the beating and hanging of the de Witt brothers under fictitious pretexts. According to rumors, during the execution, onlookers even engaged in cannibalism with the severed body parts of the brothers. Accordingly, our music is about this - about injustice, about suffering and about the atonement of sins.
