The second album by one of the main new stars of Japanese hyperpop, also a member of the hyperpop/rap trio Dr.Anon, all of whose members do not particularly talk about their real names. In his solo work, e5 more often turns to melodies than to recitatives, but strange digicor/hyperpop electronics, multiplied by Japanese sound, are at the forefront here. The wonderful Japanese zine AVYSS recently interviewed the artist for the release of her second solo album - which is not a reason to go on an exciting audio journey.
