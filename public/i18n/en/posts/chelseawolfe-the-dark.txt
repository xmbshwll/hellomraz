New album of the prima donna. Perhaps the most popular available record in the discography - flirtations with industrial and doom metal have been removed, this is very well-made pop music with a gothic touch and endlessly boring lyrics about how cool it is when you are in the dark, and when you are not in the dark - uncool. Co-produced by Grammy winner Jennifer Decilveo (Hozier, Miley Cyrus), Stella Mozgawa (Warpaint) on drums, Justin Meldal-Johnsen (NII) on bass.
