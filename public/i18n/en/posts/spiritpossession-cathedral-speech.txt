The third album of the duo from Portland, also turning to the formula of early black metal, but with signature moves into psychedelia and borrowing moves from proto-death and heavy metal. The occult part is in place (and not a reference to the right-wing waste paper of Curtis Yarvin, as someone might have thought from the title of the album), no one canceled the most talented song titles - such as WALLPAPER OF ANGELS NAILED TO A WALL, for example.
