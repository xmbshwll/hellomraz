Gabe Poirot Claims He Met Jesus While Spent 18 Days in a Coma 

An American who claims to have met Jesus after spending 18 days in a coma described “him” as “six feet tall but as tall as eternity” and “a mustang full of testosterone”. 

Gabe Poirot was riding an electric skateboard at about 30 mph without a helmet in October 2021 when he hit a small bump in the road and flipped backwards, landing directly on his head.
