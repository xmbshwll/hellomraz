New long-play Hand Model, originally a one-man electronic project of Ignat Frege from Wreck and Reference. Over time, the project grew into a full-fledged live band with the addition of Jess Labrador (Chasms), Zack Frederick and Nathan Burger (Hundred Bliss). The sound is everything that can be expected from a member of Wreck and Reference, but there are interesting changes with the label - this is samizdat, which is unexpected, the previous recordings of the group were published by Ignat’s native Flenser.
